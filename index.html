How it works

From first sketch to curb appeal

Three simple steps take your yard from an idea to a plan you can build.

  1. 01

    Consult and concept

    We walk your yard together and talk about how you want to use it. Then the big ideas get sketched out: shade, privacy, color, and a welcoming path to the door.

  2. 02

    Planting plan

    The concept becomes a scaled drawing. Every bed, path, and plant is placed with care, with a plant list you or your installer can follow.

  3. 03

    Curb appeal

    With a clear plan in hand, installation goes smoothly. You end up with a yard that looks great from the street and works for the way you live.

About

Hi, I’m Kayra.

I design landscapes for homeowners who want their yard to feel as good as the rest of their home. Some want a fresh first impression from the street. Others want a backyard they’ll actually use. Most want both, without wasting water or weekends.

Every plan starts with listening: how you live, what you love, and how much upkeep you want. From there I draw a design that fits your site, your style, and your budget.
